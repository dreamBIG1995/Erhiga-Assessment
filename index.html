Home

Sunny Farm is a family-friendly visitor attraction located between Edinburgh and Carlisle.

We offer a petting zoo, café, play park and beautiful countryside walks.

Monday - Friday: 9:00am - 5:00pm

About Us

Sunny Farm has been transformed into a welcoming place for families, tourists and day visitors.

Our aim is to provide a fun, relaxing and professional environment for everyone.

Who We Are For

  • Tourists
  • Garden lovers
